# CV and portfolio surface

## THESIS

Recruiters and technical managers can identify Vincent’s role fit, inspect evidence from real client work, and contact him without sorting through an app-like dashboard.

## OWN-WORLD

An editorial technical portfolio in forest green and warm paper. IBM Plex Sans carries the reading; a restrained serif display face gives headings a distinct voice. Hairline rules and whitespace carry the structure.

## STORY

Read my role and scope, get a broad view of how I work, scan six career indicators, then review my reverse-chronological work history and contact links.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The header identifies Vincent and offers section navigation and the PDF CV. The left column gives the role, an evidence-led introduction, location and contact/download actions. The right column summarizes his general approach through three concise points: framing the need, evolving applications, and working with the team. The first screen should make his profile and way of working clear; concrete client missions appear in the experience timeline.

## FORM

Use one numbered title per section to avoid repeating the same subject in a separate index and headline. Put the experience timeline immediately after the six-measure band, ahead of role preferences, expertise, and AI practices, to keep client evidence high in the reading order. Present the jury role and personal interests as a full-width editorial callout under education and credentials. Keep the general approach summary as three open, typographic rows, not cards; leave specific client examples in the experience timeline. Use one flat forest-green measure band with six figures and labels in three columns by two rows on wide screens; use two columns on smaller screens. Show 8+ clients as a lower bound because the history also includes unnamed client assignments. Group similar job titles into five role families. Show 10+ front-end and 15+ back-end and infrastructure skills. Keep the band free of supporting notes and methodology text. Show all expertise areas as open content. List companies and missions newest first; each mission uses native disclosure for its detail and environment. Use no entrance animation; hover and disclosure feedback stay brief and subtle, with reduced motion respected.

## QUALITY BAR

- At 1440px, the role, project evidence, and contact action are visible together without visual clutter.
- At 390px, content remains within the viewport and headings, mission dates, disclosures, and contact targets remain readable and operable.
- The strongest claims are tied to the supplied CV: three ESN employers, at least eight named client organizations with additional unnamed assignments, eleven years since September 2015, five role families after grouping similar titles, 10+ front-end skills, 15+ back-end and infrastructure skills, three Ayming projects in parallel, CNR’s WPF-to-web refonte, Legrand’s data migration for PLM, and the Private GPT front-end migration from Blazor to React.
- No client logos, measured business outcomes, backend ownership for Private GPT, testimonial, or other unsupported proof is introduced.
- The page should read as a personal technical portfolio rather than a generic SaaS dashboard or a collection of promotional cards.
